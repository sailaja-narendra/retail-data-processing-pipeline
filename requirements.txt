
## How to Run
1. Open `retail_sales_etl.ipynb` in **Google Colab**  
2. Run all cells step by step  
3. Outputs will be generated automatically in `/outputs` and in `sales_dw.db`

## Results
- Daily sales summary
- Product performance summary
- Revenue by category (via SQL queries)
